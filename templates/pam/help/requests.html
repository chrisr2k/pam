Requests & Approvals

Requesting access
  1. Go to New Request (or click Request Access on a role).
  2. Pick the role, write a justification (required — auditors read it), and choose a duration. Durations above the role's maximum are automatically reduced, with a warning.
  3. Submit. If you've enrolled in MFA, you'll confirm with a 6-digit code.
  4. Track it on My Requests or the request's detail page.
What happens next
  • Requests wait at PENDING until enough approvers vote (see quorum below).
  • Some roles don't require approval — those grant immediately and provision in the background.
  • Once approved, access is created in the cloud provider and becomes active until it expires automatically. No one has to remember to remove it.
  • You can revoke your own access early from the request page or the dashboard — do this as soon as you're done.
Approving requests
  • Requests needing your approval appear under Approvals (and on your dashboard). You must be listed as an approver of that role.
  • Approving requires MFA: enter your authenticator code on the "Verify Your Identity" page to record your vote.
  • Quorum: each role decides how many votes are needed — any one approver, all approvers, or a fixed number. The detail page shows live progress ("2 of 3 approved"). Your vote counts immediately even when more are needed.
  • Deny rejects the request outright, at any point — no quorum needed to say no.
  • One vote per approver: after voting you'll see "You approved this request" instead of the buttons.
Lifecycle cheat sheet

PENDING awaiting votes · APPROVED quorum met · PROVISIONING granting now · PROVISIONED active — expires at the shown time · DENIED declined · FAILED provisioning error (tell an admin) · EXPIRED done — access removed.
